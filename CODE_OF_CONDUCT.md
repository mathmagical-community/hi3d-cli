# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

In short: be respectful and constructive; no harassment, personal attacks or discriminatory language; assume good faith;
keep discussions on topic. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that violate these rules,
and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers (see `SECURITY.md` for the contact route). Reports are handled confidentially.
